# Tovix Brand Pack

Offline identity kit for the Tovix game studio. Use these files across Discord, Roblox, socials, and the website.

## Direction: Split Voltage

Dual-channel energy on void black. The mark’s hard center gap is the brand metaphor — precision, rivalry, balance. Glow lives on the emblem (and rare CTA edges), not as a sitewide neon wash.

## Studio bio

**1-line:** Independent studio building sharp, high-energy games under a dual-signal mark.

**~40 words:** Tovix is a game studio focused on bold interactive experiences with clean systems and strong visual identity. We ship player-first titles with precision craft—from first prototype to launch—keeping the brand’s red/cyan split as a reminder to balance intensity with clarity.

## Clear space & don’ts

- Keep empty space around the mark of at least **½ the mark height**.
- Prefer the mark on **black**. On light backgrounds, place it on a black plate.
- Do not recolor the halves, remove the center gap, or add extra glow stacks.
- Do not stretch or rotate the mark.
